    How to use this
    1. Get the chords in. Paste them into the Song box — chord lines, [C] brackets or a whole copied chord page all work. Or tap your Grab chords bookmarklet while on a chord site and it lands here automatically.
    2. Chords or Sheet. The Chords tab groups the song into sections; the Sheet tab lays out whatever you pasted — words and all — with every chord tappable where it falls in the line.
    3. Tap a chord to hear it and see it on the keyboard. Amber is the root, green are the keys to play, pale green is the same note in another octave, and the circled numbers are right-hand fingers (1 = thumb).
    4. Smooth voicing picks inversions so your hand barely moves between chords; Root position shows every chord from its root. The ‹ › arrows step through inversions by hand.
    5. Play chord sounds the shape; Play progression walks the whole song; each section has its own Play.
    6. Key and numerals. The key is detected from the chords, and each chip shows its Roman numeral — the pattern that repeats across songs. Override the key if the guess is off.
    7. Transpose with − and + to suit your voice. Numerals stay the same; chord names respell to the new key.
    8. Save to library keeps the song on this device, with its key and transposition. The star keeps a single chord. Export writes a backup file you can re-import anywhere.

    On a computer: space plays the current chord, left and right arrows step through the song, up and down change inversion.
